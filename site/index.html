A MOMENT OF PRIVACY

Tea can wait. Your save cannot.

Move with WASD or arrows. Hold Shift to sneak. E to interact. Escape to pause. On a phone, use the thumbstick and buttons. New Tuesdays begin on the silo’s upper landing: press E / Interact to descend. Explore the control room, then use the ladder to reach the forest. The concealed hatch brings you home again.

The consoles show Earth, orbital diagrams, a galaxy survey and Gary’s in-game patrol. They are fictional displays, not live astronomical data. Visit the tea table and grooming station; both restore Arthur’s composure. Follow the gold beacon to continue the Tuesday mission.

On iPhone, open the published website in Safari. After “Offline ready”, use Share → Add to Home Screen. Your first visit and updates need a connection. Clearing website data can remove your save; keep an exported backup. Existing recovery saves are supported; choose New Tuesday to see the new opening.

Procedural 3D artwork and written Arthur dialogue. This build does not include recorded voice acting or a flyable spacecraft.
