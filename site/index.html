A MOMENT OF PRIVACY

Tea can wait. Your save cannot.

Move with WASD or arrows. Hold Shift to sneak. E to interact. Escape to pause. On a phone, use the thumbstick and buttons. Follow the gold beacon; tree trunks and fern cover help you hide from Gary.

On iPhone, open the published website in Safari. After “Offline ready”, use Share → Add to Home Screen. Your first visit and updates need a connection. Clearing website data can remove your save; keep an exported backup.

This replacement uses procedural 3D parts. The previous GLB, original test results, WebAssembly module and voice recordings are not part of this build.
